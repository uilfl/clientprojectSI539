Global Information Engagement Project: Heidelberg Project

Location: Detroit, Michigan

Student participants: UMSI students

Community partner: The Heidelberg Project, a Detroit-based community arts organization.

About the experience: Students visited the Heidelberg Project as part of a community-engaged learning experience. The project connects art, place, and community participation, giving students an opportunity to learn from a Detroit organization and its local context.

Learning outcome: Students practiced listening to community perspectives, collaborating across differences, and considering how information and creative work can support local communities.

CIDEXPO Engaged Learning Experience

Location: Dearborn, Michigan

Student participants: UMSI students

Community partner: CIDEXPO organizers and participating local partners.

About the experience: Students took part in a CIDEXPO exhibition experience in Dearborn, engaging with projects and community-facing work outside the traditional classroom.

Learning outcome: Students practiced presenting ideas, communicating with public audiences, and connecting their academic learning to community and organizational needs.
